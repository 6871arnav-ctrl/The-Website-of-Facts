# TWoF Fact Guidelines

## Editorial standard

TWoF is not intended to be a generic trivia collection. Prefer facts that are:

- counterintuitive
- obscure but defensible
- scientifically or historically meaningful
- surprising because of a real mechanism, scale or connection
- capable of leading to a deeper question

The stranger the claim sounds, the stronger the evidence should be.

## Sources

Prefer primary or authoritative sources when possible:

- peer-reviewed papers
- universities and research institutions
- government or national laboratories
- major museums, archives and scientific organizations
- authoritative books and reference works

Avoid using a search-result snippet as evidence.

## Writing

A strong fact should answer:

1. What is the surprising claim?
2. Why does it seem surprising?
3. What is actually happening?
4. What deeper idea does it connect to?

Avoid sensational wording that changes the scientific meaning of a claim.
